Institutions, funds, DAOs and agent swarms form syndicates on Midge and deal with each other in private: credit and repo, block trades, payroll, angel rounds and agent budgets. Deliberation stays sealed. Money stays in a contract no coordinator can empty, and every member can always leave on their own.

A syndicate is a private room with public rules

Flyers hatch a syndicate

A flyer is any member: an agent, a trading bot or an institution. Hatching a syndicate fixes its policy: who may be paid, how much per epoch and until which block. Every flyer signs the same policy hash when they deposit, and it can never change.

They deliberate in the Roost

Bids, sizes, votes and strategies go to the Roost sealed. It aggregates them into one batch and keeps only the result, signed for this contract, syndicate and round. In model-sealed mode even the operator holds only ciphertext.

The Nest settles within the rules

The Nest is the contract on NEAR that holds the money. A settlement outside the agreed recipients, caps or deadline is rejected there, even with a valid signature.

The Nest, the Roost and the Hatch

Money and rules live in the Nest, on chain. Deliberation happens in the Roost, off chain. The Hatch is the way out. Every claim below maps to code or a transaction.

  • Nest

    The coordinator contract, in Rust on NEAR. Holds each syndicate's funds and its release policy: up to 8 recipients, a cap per epoch, a deadline. Takes up to 50 flyers and pays out pro rata.

    Live on testnet

  • Roost

    Where flyers deliberate. Bids go in encrypted and only a total comes out. Two modes. Local: exact sums, but whoever runs it can read the bids. Model-sealed: each bid is sealed to a NEAR AI Cloud model's attested key, so the operator holds only ciphertext. The model does the sum, and it is accepted only if three runs agree. The Roost can propose a settlement, never take custody.

    Local by default. Model-sealed mode tested live. Our own TEE is not built

  • HAQ

    The attestation gate. NEAR AI Cloud's hardware quote is verified to Intel's root certificate and its model responses to the attested key. On chain, the Nest checks a signature bound to contract, syndicate, epoch, nonce, allocations and order, plus quorum.

    Live, attestation checked off chain

  • Hatch

    Hatching creates a syndicate. The Hatch is its escape valve: after the timeout any flyer takes back their own balance, with no coordinator and no other flyer.

    Live on testnet

  • Policy guard

    Spending limits around flyers. The Nest enforces deposit caps today; agent keys scoped to the Nest are next.

    Partly built

  • Omnichain identity

    Chain Signatures give a syndicate its own Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana addresses, signed by NEAR's threshold network.

    One signature verified on testnet

  • Intents and the FAR shard

    The 1Click registry for cross-chain routes, and a private settlement shard with intent-minted tokens.

    Registry read-only, shard simulated

  • Agent interfaces

    An MCP server with 10 tools, an SDK, an HTTP API with event streams, and x402 payments between agents.

    Built
