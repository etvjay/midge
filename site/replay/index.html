    What this page does not prove

    • A log that checks out was not changed after it was written. It does not show the events were honestly produced, so the transactions are linked for you to open on the explorer, and the settlement is fetched live from the chain.
    • The hardware's attestation is in each run as a recorded event. Checking the Intel certificate chain needs the verifier behind npm run verify:tee, which this page does not repeat.
    • Amounts and addresses are public on NEAR. The paper trail hides who each address belongs to, and a person with a unique and unchanged amount can still be matched across months.
    • Cy's missing tax was planted to show the review. It shows the reviewer catching a kind of mistake its brief names, not every real one.
    • One operator ran every account in these tests. No independent operators have been shown, and nothing here runs on mainnet.
