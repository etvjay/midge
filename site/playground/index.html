    Playground

    Approve a pay run yourself

    You approve a real pay run on NEAR testnet. A model on NEAR AI Cloud reviews it first, a program in a secure hardware enclave works out the payments and signs them, and the NEAR contract pays them, including your own line, to an account that only you hold the key to. Every result is checked in your browser.

    What a pay run does not show

    • A member leaving alone with their own money. If the room stops, any member calls the exit and the contract pays back what is left of their share, with no operator or room signature. In a recorded run on real hardware the room stalled and a member did exactly that: the exit on NearBlocks · the recorded run.
    • Several members deciding together, in private. A buyer asks three invited makers for a price, each maker seals a quote, and a public rule picks the winner: replay the sealed quotes. A vote, a block trade and a pledge pool are on the Replay page too.

    What this does not prove

    • The service that runs the round is the employer and the coordinator. It chooses you as the approver for your round, and the group's public rules don't fix the approver yet, so the contract doesn't see your approval. Only the room checks it.
    • The service, and not the room or the contract, makes sure that a model reviewed exactly the lines you send. The model's own hardware attestation is recorded but not checked on this page.
    • Everything here uses NEAR testnet, where the coins have no value. One operator runs the service, the room and every other account, and one round runs at a time.
    • Amounts and addresses are public on NEAR. The room keeps who each address belongs to, and the sealed payslips are the only record of that link.
