## What changed since 2026-11-07

- **A playground anyone can run.** A visitor edits a real pay run, a model on NEAR AI Cloud reviews it (its signed receipt is checked in the browser, and the service only starts a round for exactly the reviewed lines), approves with a key made in their browser, the room in a TDX machine signs, the contract settles on testnet, and the visitor opens their own payslip. It was the first item on the 2026-11-07 list.
- **More programs in the hardware room:** sealed quotes (three runs), block trades with a minimum fill (two), a ballot and a decision-only vote, and pay runs with an AI review and a second approver, all live on Phala TDX.
- **Contract-signed foreign payments:** the contract checked a group's rules before NEAR Chain Signatures signed payments on Solana devnet and Ethereum Sepolia, and refused three that broke the rules.
- **Replay:** six use cases from recorded runs, re-checked in the browser.
- **Engineering hygiene:** Biome lint, an opt-in strict type check, dead-code and duplicate checks, coverage gates, CodeQL, gitleaks and cargo-deny in CI; room-signer tests; the unused SDK removed, which cleared npm audit.
- **Operations:** the room reads the chain through a private endpoint as well as a pinned public one; the lab cleanup job deleted the playground's machines once (GAP-058), and they were restored and protected the same day.
