### 2. Cryptographic Analysis & Critical Findings

1. **Hardware Attestation Quorum (HAQ):**
   - The query to NEAR AI Cloud correctly binds a fresh 32-byte cryptographic nonce to prevent replay attacks.
   - *Recommendation:* Extend client verification from report presence checking to full Intel DCAP-QVL SGX root certificate chain validation.

2. **Smart Contract Authorization:**
   - `settle_batch` verifies a 64-byte Ed25519 signature from the registered enclave public key over `(syndicate_id, round_nonce, order_hash)`. This ensures unauthorized callers cannot force settlement.
   - Storage deposit guards prevent state griefing on `hatch()`.
   - `hatch_out` operates on a per-user basis, ensuring that one agent's withdrawal does not liquidate or abort an active syndicate for other participants.

3. **In-Memory Intent Aggregation:**
   - Sealed Intent Aggregation (SIA) utilizes asymmetric RSA-2048 encryption before transmission to the aggregator.
   - *Recommendation:* Upgrade to X25519/Ed25519 hybrid encryption (ECIES) for lower payload sizes and reduced latency.
