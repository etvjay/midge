## Intent

A cinematic product film for Midge that comes before the demo video. It says what
Midge is, the idea it rests on, and what it brings to NEAR. Calm, exact and
typographic, in the feel of the ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Realtime launch film the user
referenced: mostly paper and ink, colour only at moments of meaning, type doing the
storytelling, one small diagram per idea, gentle 3D camera drift, cuts hidden in
morphs. Relief at the exit is the emotional peak.

## Assets

- ../../site/favicon.svg — the Midge mark (a cluster of dots, one leaving on a curved path); the film's through-line.

## Customizations

- No narrator. On-screen text, a music bed and sound effects carry everything. Lines hold long enough to be spoken so a voice can be added later as a separate layer.
- Sound: a warm, minimal underscore (soft pad, slow pulse, sparse piano or bell) that sits behind everything; tactile physical effects on every motion (paper, soft clicks, a wooden knock, a door); the bed dips under each effect. Two near-silences: as the door closes, and just before the exit. Music and effects from HeyGen.
- Two worked examples: a sealed auction (followed start to finish), then payroll.
- The three hosts get their own beat: our program on Phala, a model on NEAR AI Cloud, or two hosts that must both sign. The local simulation is not shown.
- Members (AI agents, funds, businesses, people) are drawn identically and named equally.
- Phala, NEAR AI Cloud and Chain Signatures appear as plain text, never logos.
- Ends on the mark, "Private rooms with public rules" and "Live on NEAR testnet" (the website address is hidden for now, at the user's request).

## Notes

- Source of truth for wording: docs/GLOSSARY.md and docs/WHITEPAPER.md. Plain words only: no TEE, attestation, nonce, hatch_out, syndicate, Roost, Nest, "trustless".
- Roles: Phala hosts our program (the room). NEAR AI Cloud reasons and advises (the advisor sees only the total) and is also a second kind of host (inputs sealed to a model's key). The NEAR contract holds the money and the rules and gives the exit. Chain Signatures pays on other chains.
- Never say or imply amounts are private. Payroll is "private who, with private receipts"; amounts are public.
- No figures, test counts or run ids on screen. No "prototype with no token" line.
- Rooms on the network are shown separate; they do not interact yet.
- Colour: paper #eef0ec, ink #15181a, lake #1f5f5b; signals only for meaning: room teal #7cc4b8, a member's own share #d9824a, refused #e0675a. Type: Newsreader (display), Geist (body), Geist Mono (labels).
